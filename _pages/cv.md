## Research Thrusts

My doctoral research investigates **closed-loop reliability in physical robot interaction** across four interconnected pillars:

1. **Active Perception & Visual Servoing**: Dual-arm viewpoint optimization and target–gripper relative visual servoing under self-occlusion and environmental obstruction.
2. **Pre-Execution Vision-Language Verification**: Closed-loop Verify-and-Refine visual grounding and geometric mask projection for dual-arm deformable object manipulation.
3. **Visuotactile Policy Adaptation**: Cross-modal fusion of vision and tactile force fields with reinforcement learning for contact-rich insertion tasks.
4. **Failure Recovery & Episodic Memory**: Online value/risk assessment, reflexive retraction feedback, and experience memory retrieval after execution anomalies.
