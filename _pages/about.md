I am **Zhilun Hu (胡至伦)**, a Ph.D. student at **Huazhong University of Science and Technology**, advised by **Prof. [Weiming Shen](https://mse.hust.edu.cn/info/1143/1385.htm)**.

My research focuses on **reliable robotic manipulation**: improving what robots observe, checking the actions they propose, and adapting their behavior during physical interaction. My interests span active visual servoing, dual-arm deformable-object manipulation, visuotactile policy learning, and failure recovery.

我是胡至伦，华中科技大学博士研究生，导师为沈卫明教授。我的研究围绕机器人操作的可靠性展开，关注主动视觉伺服、双臂柔性物体操作、视觉触觉策略学习，以及故障恢复与经验记忆。

## Research interests

- **Active perception and visual servoing** for manipulation under occlusion.
- **Vision-language reasoning** for dual-arm and deformable-object manipulation.
- **Visuotactile policy adaptation and reinforcement learning** for contact-rich tasks.
- **Failure recovery and experience memory** for robot policies.
