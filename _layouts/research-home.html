Zhilun Hu 胡至伦

Ph.D. Student in Mechanical Engineering (Robotics & Embodied AI)
School of Mechanical Science and Engineering, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)

I am a Ph.D. student at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), advised by Prof. Weiming Shen (IEEE Fellow, Academician of CAE). My research centers on reliable robotic manipulation—investigating how physical agents actively acquire unoccluded observations, verify high-level action proposals against geometric constraints prior to execution, adapt policies through visuotactile interaction, and recover from execution anomalies.

我是华中科技大学机械科学与工程学院博士研究生，师从沈卫明教授。我的研究致力于解决机器人操作中的可靠性问题（Reliable Robotic Manipulation），构建从主动视觉伺服感知、双臂柔性操作先验视觉校验、视触觉强化学习策略自适应到故障恢复与经验记忆的完整闭环架构。
