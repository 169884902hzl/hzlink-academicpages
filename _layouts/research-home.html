Zhilun Hu 胡至伦

Ph.D. Candidate in Mechanical Engineering · School of Mechanical Science and Engineering, HUST
Advised by Prof. Weiming Shen IEEE Fellow · CAE Academician

My research focuses on Reliable Robotic Manipulation—investigating how physical agents actively acquire unoccluded visual observations, pre-verify high-level action proposals against geometric constraints, adapt contact-rich policies through visuotactile reinforcement learning, and recover from execution anomalies.

研究主线：华中科技大学机械科学与工程学院博士研究生，师从沈卫明教授。致力于解决机器人操作中的物理可靠性问题，构建从主动视觉伺服感知、双臂柔性操作先验视觉校验、视触觉强化学习策略自适应到反射式故障恢复与情景经验记忆的闭环智能体架构。
