About Me

I am Zhilun Hu (胡至伦), a Ph.D. student in the School of Mechanical Science and Engineering at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Weiming Shen (IEEE Fellow, Academician of the Canadian Academy of Engineering).

My research focuses on Reliable Robotic Manipulation. I investigate how embodied robotic agents can actively acquire unoccluded visual feedback, verify high-level action proposals against geometric constraints prior to execution, adapt contact-rich policies via visuotactile sensing and reinforcement learning, and recover from anomalous execution states.

我是胡至伦，华中科技大学机械科学与工程学院博士研究生，师从沈卫明教授（IEEE Fellow、加拿大工程院院士）。我的研究聚焦于机器人可靠操作（Reliable Robotic Manipulation），致力于构建涵盖双臂主动视觉伺服感知、柔性物体操作执行前视觉校验、视触觉多模态强化学习自适应以及反射式故障恢复与经验记忆的闭环智能操作体系。

Research Interests

  • Active Perception & Visual Servoing: Dual-arm active viewpoint planning and target–gripper relative visual servoing under occlusion.
  • Vision-Language Robotic Manipulation: Iterative visual-overlay action verification and 3D geometric constraint reasoning for deformable objects.
  • Visuotactile Policy Learning: Cross-modal visuotactile fusion and reinforcement learning policy adaptation for contact-rich manipulation.
  • Failure Recovery & Experience Memory: Online value/risk monitoring, reflexive retraction control, and episodic memory retrieval.
