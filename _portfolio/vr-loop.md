**Manuscript under review · Second-author paper**

Rope untangling requires reasoning about complex geometry: an unsuitable grasp or pull may tighten a tangle. VR-Loop treats the proposed interaction points and pulling directions as actions to be checked and refined before execution.

The framework combines visual-language reasoning, image-overlay verification, mask-based constraints, and depth-informed geometric execution for dual-arm rope manipulation.

## Manuscript

**Iterative Verify-and-Refine Vision-Language Agent for Reliable Dual-Arm Manipulation**  
Yuqi Cheng, **Zhilun Hu**, Cheng Jiang, Qingyu Peng, Weiming Shen.
