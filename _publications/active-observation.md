## Abstract & Method Overview

During terminal robotic grasping, the manipulator or gripper frequently obscures the target object just when closed-loop visual feedback is most critical. This work proposes a dual-arm active observation framework in which one manipulator actively positions an eye-in-hand RGB-D camera while the second manipulator executes the grasp. By combining a two-stage viewpoint strategy, target–gripper relative image error feedback, and occlusion-triggered viewpoint exploration, the system preserves or recovers clear co-visibility throughout physical interaction.
